Natural Earth publishes `POP_EST` and `GDP_MD` as its own **estimates**, each with the
year it refers to in `POP_YEAR` / `GDP_YEAR`. They are copied as they are: not adjusted,
not annualised, not re-estimated. `GDP_MD` is recorded in millions of US dollars, and the
pipeline multiplies by 1,000,000 and records that it did (`source_scale`).
